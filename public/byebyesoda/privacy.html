Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 22, 2026 • Version 1.0

In short: ByeBye Soda is built on a strict offline-first, device-only architecture. We do not collect, track, share, or monetize your personal information.

1. Introduction

ByeBye Soda ("the App", "we", "us", or "our") is created and maintained by Marvin Aquino. This Privacy Policy outlines our uncompromising commitment to your privacy when you use our mobile application.

2. What the App Stores On Your Device

All activity data you record within ByeBye Soda remains strictly on your local device storage. This includes:

  • Daily drink intake and reduction logs
  • Calculated metrics (sugar avoided and money saved)
  • Personal milestone targets and wishlist goals
  • User interface preferences and settings

You are never required to register an account, sign in via third-party providers, or provide personal credentials.

3. What We Collect

Zero personal data collection. We do not operate remote tracking servers, and we do not collect:

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers, or account logins
  • Location or GPS information
  • Device identifiers, advertising IDs, or cross-app tracking beacons
  • Third-party analytics or crash telemetry SDKs

4. Third Parties and Network Access

The App does not sell, trade, license, or transmit your data to any third party. The App does not contain third-party ad networks or programmatic bidding SDKs.

5. Data Deletion and Portability

Because all application data is stored locally on your device:

  • You can clear your log history or reset the app directly within the app settings.
  • Uninstalling the application from your device permanently removes all associated database records.

6. Children’s Privacy

ByeBye Soda is not targeted toward children under the age of 13. Consistent with our zero-data policy, we do not knowingly request, collect, or store personal information from children or any other users.

7. Health and Nutritional Information

ByeBye Soda is an informational habit-tracking tool. It is not intended to provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Sugar calculations and nutritional values are estimates intended for motivational habit tracking. Always consult a certified healthcare professional for dietary advice.

8. Policy Updates

If we make updates to this Privacy Policy, the revised version will be published here with an updated "Effective Date".
